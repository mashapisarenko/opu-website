What conductors and university faculty have written about me

These are not quotes I typed out. Each one is a letter on the institution’s own letterhead, signed. Click any letter to read the whole thing. The only marks on them are gray rectangles where a private home address or a direct phone number used to be — mine and theirs. Nothing else has been touched.

“On February 14th Masha performed brilliantly, eliciting an instant standing ovation from the 1,200 in attendance and playing the Concerto as if it had been part of her repertory for many years.”

He invited her as guest soloist for Rachmaninov’s Second Piano Concerto — a work, he writes, “she had not yet studied or performed.” Earlier in the same letter: “an exceptionally gifted pianist” whose “ease, fluidity, and poetic interpretation” reminded him of Valentina Lisitsa.

Harold Weller · 2010
Founding Music Director & Conductor Laureate, The Las Vegas Philharmonic
Read the letter itself →

“One of our finest piano majors commented enthusiastically … and noted that this may have been the best recital he has ever attended. Another audience member perceived each note as having a life of its own and noted Ms. Pisarenko’s ability to make the notes ‘dance with each other.’”

After a recital and a masterclass with four piano majors: “The students appreciated Ms. Pisarenko’s musical insights, technical advice, as well as her patient and helpful demeanor.”

Dr. Christian Bohnenstengel, NCTM · 2012

“Ms. Pisarenko is also in charge of teaching some of our music Piano Major students and for preparing them for recitals and juries. In this task she has always done a remarkable work. Her students demonstrate solid technic and musicianship.”

Teaching and recognition

  • University faculty — College of Southern Nevada and Southern Utah University
  • D.M.A. in Piano Performance, University of Nevada Las Vegas — and a second doctorate, a Ph.D. in Linguistics and English-language teaching, whose dissertation was on how children who learn differently are taught. I do not teach in my first language by accident; I studied how to do it.
  • My own students have placed in the Silver State and Legacy competitions, 2016–2019
  • My students have taken Distinctions in ABRSM examinations and played at the ABRSM High Scorers’ Concert in 2025 and again in 2026 — the letters are above
