Cables are the least glamorous part of a music corner and the part that most often fails. Most beginners need only a handful: a cable from the piano's outputs to an interface or speakers, a USB cable to the computer, a headphone adapter, and a surge protector. I choose plain, well-made cables, and I ask you to check the plug on each end against your own gear before you order.

Why trust a concert pianist's picks?

I recommend this gear because it is what my adult students need to work through my free 41-lesson course and, later, to use with my upcoming method books. I am a concert pianist and a university piano professor, and these are the kinds of tools I look at first when a student asks what to buy.

What should I look for in music cables?

  • The plug on each end. Look at your piano, interface or speakers and match 1/4-inch TRS, XLR, 3.5 mm, USB-B, USB-C or five-pin MIDI exactly.
  • Balanced or unbalanced. A balanced TRS or XLR cable keeps noise out over longer runs.
  • Length. Buy the length you need, not the longest; a long extra loop picks up hum.
  • Strain relief. A cable that bends sharply at the plug fails there first; look for a molded plug with a spring or boot.
  • Surge protection. Count your outlets and check the protector's ratings in the listing.

How do good cables help you?

How this helps you learn: the right cables get your playing onto a recording without noise, and a recording you can trust is how you hear your own progress.

What do players ask about cables and power?

What cable connects a digital piano to an audio interface?

Usually two 1/4-inch cables, one for left and one for right, from the piano's line outputs to the interface's line inputs. Check your piano's output jacks first.

Do I need an expensive cable?

No. A plain cable from a known maker with solid plugs is enough. Expensive cables do not make a digital piano sound better.

What is a USB-B cable used for?

Many digital pianos, interfaces and MIDI keyboards have a square USB-B port. A USB-B to USB-C cable connects them to a modern laptop.

Do I need a surge protector?

A surge protector is a cheap way to protect a piano and a computer. Plug the piano's power supply into it and check the listing for what it covers.
