How do I choose a piano as an adult?

I choose in this order. First the keys: weighted keys that answer differently to a soft and a loud touch. Second the sound, which should be a real piano sound before it is anything else. Third the pedals, because the sustain pedal is part of the instrument. Fourth the room: measure where it will stand, and decide whether a portable on a stand or a cabinet suits it. Last the extras, which are a bench at the right height and a pair of headphones.

Portable, console, hybrid or grand?

A portable digital piano is the practical start. A home console or upright-style cabinet is the piano to keep. A hybrid or wooden-key instrument is for a player who has outgrown the first touch, and a digital grand is a piano-shaped statement for a room that has space. A stage piano is for playing out. For acoustic pianos I send adults to a local dealer, where you can hear and touch the instrument.

What do adults ask before buying a piano?

What is the best digital piano for an adult beginner?

One with 88 weighted keys, a real piano sound, a sustain pedal and a headphone jack. After that, choose the one you can afford and fit in the room. My long answer is in the guide on which digital piano an adult beginner should buy.

What is the difference between a digital piano, a hybrid piano and a digital grand?

A digital piano makes its sound electronically with weighted keys. A hybrid adds a wooden-key hammer mechanism for a touch closer to an acoustic piano. A digital grand puts digital technology inside a grand-piano shape. All three have a volume control and a headphone jack.

Can I buy an acoustic upright or grand piano on Amazon?

I searched Amazon by brand and model for uprights and grands on November 14, 2026 and found digital instruments and accessories, but no acoustic piano I could verify as sold and shipped there. An acoustic piano should be played, delivered by movers and tuned, so I suggest a local piano dealer, and I list the digital models that Amazon does sell.

Should I buy a digital piano or a keyboard?

A digital piano if you can: weighted keys are what teach touch. A keyboard if space or budget is tight, chosen with touch-sensitive keys and planned as a stepping stone.

What else do I need with a digital piano?

A sustain pedal, an adjustable bench, headphones and a stand if the piano has no legs. I list each in its own shop page.
