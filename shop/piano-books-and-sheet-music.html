Why I recommend this gear

I recommend this gear because it is what my adult students need to work through my free 41-lesson course and, later, to use with my upcoming method books. I am a concert pianist and a university piano professor, and these are the kinds of tools I look at first when a student asks what to buy.

How I choose

  • Written for adults, not a children's book with the pictures removed.
  • Clear, readable print. You will read it for months.
  • It teaches reading, not only finger positions.
  • Real music by real composers as soon as possible.
  • One book at a time. Finish before you add another.

How this connects to your learning

How this helps you learn: it keeps your study in order. These are books I would choose beside the free course while my upcoming method books are being prepared.

Questions adults ask

Which method book should an adult beginner choose?

Choose one written for adults and work through it page by page. My article on choosing a method book gives ten questions to ask before you buy.

Which book do you teach from?

On my books and gear page I name the book I teach from, Alfred's Basic Adult All-in-One Course, Level 1, along with other recommendations.

Do I need a theory book as well?

A little theory helps, and a workbook gives you practice outside the piano. Keep it in step with your playing rather than ahead of it.

When should I buy Hanon or Czerny?

Technique studies suit a student who already reads and plays comfortably. Ask your teacher before you start, or finish your first method book first.
