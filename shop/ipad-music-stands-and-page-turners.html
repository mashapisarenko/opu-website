Many adults now read their music from an iPad or a tablet. It is light, it holds a whole library, and it can be bright enough for a dim room. Two things make it work at the piano: a holder that keeps the tablet at the right height and angle, and a pedal that turns the page without lifting a hand. I describe each by its listing and ask you to check the size of your tablet and the app you use before you order.

What should I look for in a tablet stand?

  • Holder type. A clamp for a microphone stand, a floor stand with a ball head, or a mount for a music stand; match it to the furniture you already have.
  • Tablet size. Check the range of sizes the holder fits, and whether your tablet's case still fits.
  • Stability. A tablet that wobbles while you play distracts; choose a firm base and a locking joint.
  • Pedal compatibility. A page turner must work with your music app; check the listing and the app's own help page.
  • Silent or clicking pedals. A silent pedal suits a recital or a quiet room.

How this helps you learn: music at eye level keeps your head and shoulders relaxed, and turning pages with your foot keeps both hands on the keys.

How do I set up a tablet at the piano?

Put the tablet where the music rest would be, tilted back so you read without lowering your head. Turn the screen's auto-lock off and keep the brightness comfortable. Pair a page-turner pedal over Bluetooth, and test the page turn on a piece you know before you need it in front of an audience.

Keep a paper copy for a recital

Batteries and Bluetooth connections fail at the worst time, so I suggest keeping a paper copy of any piece you will play for others.

What do readers ask about iPad music stands?

What do I need to read sheet music from an iPad?

A tablet holder or stand that keeps the screen at eye level, a music app that opens your scores, and optionally a Bluetooth page-turner pedal.

Do page-turner pedals work with every music app?

No. Many apps support Bluetooth pedals, but you should check your app's help page and the pedal's listing before buying.

Can I use a microphone stand for a tablet?

Yes. A tablet holder that clamps onto a microphone stand is a common solution, if the stand is heavy enough to hold the tablet without tipping.

Is a tablet better than paper music?

It is lighter and easier to carry, and your whole library is in one place. Paper is easier to mark by hand and does not run out of battery. Many players use both.
