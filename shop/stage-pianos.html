A stage piano is a different tool from a home digital piano. It is built to be carried, to connect to an amplifier or a mixer, and to give a performer many sounds. Some have no speakers at all. I include them because adults who return to piano sometimes want to play in a band, in church or at a party, and because a few of these keyboards have excellent actions. The notes below follow each listing.

Why trust a concert pianist's picks?

I recommend this gear because it is what my adult students need to work through my free 41-lesson course and, later, to use with my upcoming method books. I am a concert pianist and a university piano professor, and these are the kinds of tools I look at first when a student asks what to buy.

What should I look for in a stage piano?

  • The action. Weighted 88-key actions are the point of the category.
  • Speakers or no speakers. Many stage pianos have none, so plan for headphones or an amplifier.
  • Sounds beyond the piano. Electric piano, organ and synthesizer sounds suit a band, not practice.
  • Weight and size if you will carry it.
  • Pedal and stand. You need both, and a sustain pedal is often included.

How does a stage piano help you learn?

How this helps you learn: if your goal is to play with others, a stage piano puts a good weighted action in a form you can carry to a rehearsal.

What do players ask about stage pianos?

What is the difference between a stage piano and a digital piano?

A stage piano is made for performing and is often without speakers; a home digital piano is made for a living room, with speakers, a stand and pedals.

Do I need a stage piano as a beginner?

No. An adult beginner is better served by a digital piano with speakers and a stand. Choose a stage piano if you plan to perform with a band.

Can I practice on a stage piano with headphones?

Yes, if it has a headphone output, which the listings below describe. Check the jack size and bring an adapter if needed.

What else do I need with a stage piano?

A stand, a sustain pedal, headphones or an amplifier, and a bench at the right height. My other shop pages cover each.
