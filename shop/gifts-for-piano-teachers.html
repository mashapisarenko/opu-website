What do students ask about teacher gifts?

What is a good gift for a music teacher with many students?

Something they use at work: a music mug, a stand light for the studio, a music-note bracelet or a book on music and the brain.

Where do the gift certificates fit in?

If the person you are buying for would rather learn than own more things, give online piano lessons with me. Gift certificates bought by December 31 keep today's lesson price; prices increase January 1, 2027.

Do I need to buy anything before starting lessons?

No. My 41-lesson beginner course is free, and so is feedback on your playing. Gear only helps once the habit is there.

Are these affiliate links?

Yes. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases, at no extra cost to you. I list only items I would be glad to see in a student's home.
