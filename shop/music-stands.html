A piano has its own music rest, so why would a pianist buy a music stand? Because the rest is often too small for an open album, because a tablet score needs a steady holder, and because many adults also sing, accompany or practice away from the piano. I list folding stands, solid-desk symphony stands and a tablet floor stand, each described only by its own listing.

I recommend this gear because it is what my adult students need to work through my free 41-lesson course and, later, to use with my upcoming method books. I am a concert pianist and a university piano professor, and these are the kinds of tools I look at first when a student asks what to buy.

What should I look for in a music stand?

  • Stability. The page should not move when you turn it.
  • Height that brings the music to eye level, so your neck stays free.
  • A solid desk or a wire frame. A solid desk holds a heavy album; a wire frame folds small.
  • Portability, if you carry it to lessons or put it away after practice.
  • Tablet support, if you read scores from an iPad.

How does a music stand help you practice?

How this helps you learn: reading at eye level. A stand that holds the page steady keeps your neck free and your eyes on the music instead of on a sliding book.

What do players ask about music stands?

Do I need a music stand if my piano has a music rest?

Usually not for one book. A separate stand helps when the rest is too small for an open album, when you read from a tablet, or when you sing or practice another instrument.

What is the most stable kind of music stand?

A solid-desk orchestral stand, such as the Manhasset models listed here, is built to stay put. Folding wire stands are lighter and easier to carry.

Which music stand suits a small apartment?

A folding stand that packs away after practice. Several listings below describe folding or portable designs.

Can a music stand hold an iPad?

A floor stand made for tablets, like the elitehood listing, holds a tablet at reading height. Check your tablet's size against the listing.
