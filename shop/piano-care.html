An instrument lasts longer when it is kept clean and the room is stable. Dust covers are inexpensive, a hygrometer shows what your room is doing, and a proper polish suits a gloss finish. I list each item as its listing describes it and leave the fine print to the product page.

What does a piano need to stay in good shape?

  • The right size. A cover for an 88-key keyboard should say so.
  • A soft material that will not scratch the keys or finish.
  • A way to see humidity. A hygrometer shows what the room does through the seasons.
  • A finish-appropriate polish. Gloss pianos need a polish made for gloss.
  • Compatibility. Humidifier treatments belong to specific systems.

How does piano care help you practice?

How this helps you learn: an instrument that is clean, covered and ready to play makes it easier to sit down every day.

What do owners ask about piano care?

Should I cover my keyboard keys?

A dust cover keeps dust off the keys and the music rest area when the instrument is not in use. Choose one that fits your keyboard.

Why does a piano room need a hygrometer?

A stable room is better for a piano than a swinging one, and a hygrometer shows you what your room actually does.

Can I use household polish on a gloss piano?

I would not. Use a kit made for gloss finishes, as the MusicNomad listing describes.

Do digital pianos need care too?

Less than acoustic pianos, but dust and spills on the keys are still worth preventing.
