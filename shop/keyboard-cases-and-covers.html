What should I look for in a keyboard case?

  • The inside dimensions. Compare them with your keyboard, not just its key count.
  • Padding and straps for a bag you will carry on your back.
  • Wheels and latches for a case you will roll or ship.
  • A cutout for the music stand on a dust cover, if you leave the stand on.
  • Where it lives. A cover for the living room, a case for the car.

How does a good case help you practice?

How this helps you learn: a protected, ready instrument is easier to start every day, and a case makes lessons away from home possible.

What do owners ask about keyboard cases?

How do I know if a case fits my keyboard?

Measure your keyboard's length, width and height and compare them with the interior size on the listing. Key count alone is not enough, because cabinets differ.

Gig bag or hard case?

A gig bag is lighter and cheaper to carry to lessons. A hard case protects better for air travel and frequent moves.

Do I need a dust cover?

If your keyboard stays out in a room, yes. Dust works into keys over time, and a cover is the simplest protection.

Can I fly with a digital piano?

A hard flight case with foam and latches is built for transport, but check your airline's rules for size and weight before you book.
