Quick answer

To start piano in 2027 you need a keyboard with weighted keys if you can have them (61 touch-sensitive keys will carry a first year), a bench at the right height, closed-back headphones and a sustain pedal. Then you need a plan and someone to hear you play. This page gives you both.

A new year is a good moment to begin something you have wanted for a long time. If one of your dreams is to sit down and play, 2027 can be the year it starts. You do not need talent, a childhood of lessons or an expensive instrument. You need a keyboard, a small daily habit and a little guidance, and I will help with all three.

What does your first month of piano look like?

Four weeks, fifteen to twenty minutes a day. This is the plan I would give an adult who is learning alone.

  1. Week 1: Set up and sit down. Place the keyboard where you will see it every day and set your bench so your forearms are level with the keys. Watch Lesson 1 of my free course, then play for ten minutes. The only goal is to do it again tomorrow.
  2. Week 2: Find your hands. Learn where middle C is and place both hands around it. Fifteen minutes a day, slowly, with the lessons in order. Skip nothing; the order is what makes it easy.
  3. Week 3: Play a real tune. Choose the first short piece from the course and count it out loud. Practice it in small pieces, hands separately first, and stop each session while it still feels good.
  4. Week 4: Record and send. Record yourself on your phone and send me the recording; I read it and tell you what I hear, at no charge. Then decide what comes next: the free course alone, or private lessons with me.

What do adults ask before they start piano in 2027?

Is 2027 too late to start piano in my 40s, 50s or 60s?

No. Adults learn differently from children, not worse: you can concentrate, you understand why a technique works and you can practice on purpose. I teach adults of every age, and the ones who improve are the ones who practice a little every day.

Should I start with a keyboard or a digital piano?

If you can, start with a digital piano that has 88 weighted keys, because that touch is what you will keep learning on. A 61-key touch-sensitive keyboard will carry a first year if space or budget is tight. Avoid a keyboard with tiny keys or no touch response.

How long should I practice each day?

Fifteen to twenty minutes a day is better than one long session a week. Short, regular practice builds the habit and lets your hands remember between sessions.

Can I learn piano with online lessons as an adult?

Yes. Online lessons work well for adults: you play at your own piano, I hear and see your hands, and we work on what you actually need. My free 41-lesson course is a good way to begin before private lessons.

What is a good gift for someone starting piano in 2027?

A gift certificate for online piano lessons, or a keyboard with a bench and headphones to go with it. The gift is the beginning: a teacher and an instrument ready on the first day.
