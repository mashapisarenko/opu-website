Studio monitors are speakers made to tell the truth about what you play. A digital piano's built-in speakers are made to sound pleasant; monitors are made to show you the unevenness of your touch and your rhythm, which is what a teacher listens for. They are also the right speakers for a desk where you record, listen back and work on music. I list monitors by what each listing says, and I note where a listing sells one speaker instead of a pair.

What should I look for in studio monitors?

  • One speaker or a pair. Several monitors are sold singly, so read the title and buy two for stereo.
  • Size for the room. A small desk and a small room suit 3- to 5-inch monitors; a bigger speaker in a small room muddies the sound.
  • The inputs. Look for balanced 1/4-inch or XLR inputs if you will connect an interface, and check what cable you need.
  • Placement controls. Some monitors have switches that adjust the sound for a wall or a desk, which matters in a small room.
  • Power and size. Powered monitors plug into a wall; check the space behind and beside the desk.

How do monitors help you learn?

How this helps you learn: speakers that tell the truth let you hear what a teacher hears, the unevenness in your touch that built-in speakers politely hide.

Where do I put studio monitors?

Place the two speakers and your head at the corners of an equal-sided triangle, with the tweeters at ear height and angled toward you. Keep them a little away from the wall behind them. If they sit on a desk, a pair of foam pads or small stands from my accessories page stops the desk from buzzing.

Give your ears a day

Play a recording you know well. Honest monitors may sound less flattering than the speakers you are used to, and that is the point.

What do pianists ask about studio monitors?

What is the difference between studio monitors and ordinary speakers?

Studio monitors aim for a flat, accurate sound so you hear what is really in a recording. Ordinary speakers often add bass and sparkle to flatter the music.

Can I connect studio monitors to a digital piano?

Yes, if the piano has line outputs. Connect them with balanced or instrument cables of the right plug type; the cables page in my shop lists the common ones.

Do I need monitors if I use headphones?

No. Headphones are better for quiet practice. Monitors help when you want to hear the room, play for a listener, or mix a recording.

What size studio monitor should I buy?

A 3- to 5-inch pair for a desk in a small room, and a 5- to 8-inch pair for a larger room. A bigger speaker is not better in a small room.
