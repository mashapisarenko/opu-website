Which Digital Piano Should an Adult Beginner Buy?

Short answer: an 88-key digital piano with weighted keys, a real piano sound and a sustain pedal. Here is how to choose among them.

The short answer: buy the best 88-key digital piano with weighted keys that your budget allows, add a sustain pedal, an adjustable bench and headphones, and begin.

Start with the keys, not the features

A digital piano is mostly a keyboard. The way a key resists your finger and returns decides what your hand learns. Weighted keys answer differently to a light touch and to the weight of the arm; that difference is musical playing. Features such as hundreds of sounds, rhythm patterns and apps are secondary.

Then the sound, the pedal and the furniture

Choose a piano whose basic piano sound you enjoy listening to for an hour. Make sure a sustain pedal is included or supported, and plan for a stable stand and an adjustable bench, because sitting height changes how you sound. See the benches and stands page and my article on bench height and posture.

Eighty-eight keys or fewer?

If you are buying once, buy 88. My article on how many keys you need gives the longer answer. For the digital-versus-acoustic question, read this comparison.

My advice in order

  1. Decide where the piano will live.
  2. Choose 88 weighted keys if you can.
  3. Add a piano-style sustain pedal.
  4. Set the bench so your forearms are level with the keys.
  5. Buy closed-back headphones for daily practice.

Questions adults ask

Which digital piano should an adult beginner buy?

An 88-key digital piano with weighted keys, a good piano sound and a sustain pedal. Within that, buy the best touch your budget allows.

Are semi-weighted keys good enough?

They can carry a first year, but weighted or hammer-action keys build a touch that transfers to any piano.

Do I need a bench?

Yes. An adjustable bench sets your forearms at the right height, which a dining chair rarely does.

Can I start on a keyboard and upgrade later?

Yes, if a touch-sensitive keyboard is all that fits now. Plan to upgrade when you start reading real repertoire.
