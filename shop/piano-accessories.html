What do new owners ask about piano accessories?

What accessories do I need for a digital piano?

A piano-style sustain pedal, an adjustable bench, closed-back headphones and a light for the page. Those four change how you practice. Everything else can wait.

Does a digital piano come with a pedal?

Many include a small switch pedal and some include none. Check your listing, and plan to upgrade to a piano-style pedal for a more natural feel under the foot.

Do I need a stand for my keyboard?

If it has no legs, yes, and the stand must not wobble. My benches and stands page explains what to look for.

Which piano accessories make good gifts?

A stand light, a book clip, a metronome or headphones. Each is useful from the first week, and only the pedal has to suit a specific instrument.

Can I buy these accessories from outside the US?

The links go to Amazon.com, and Amazon ships many items internationally. Check delivery to your country on the listing before you order.
