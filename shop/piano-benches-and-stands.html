A bench will do more for how you sound than the difference between two instruments at the same price. Almost every fault I see in adult beginners starts with sitting too low, and a dining chair is almost always too low. A stand that does not wobble matters just as much, because a hand that feels the instrument move learns not to use weight.

Why trust a concert pianist's picks?

I recommend this gear because it is what my adult students need to work through my free 41-lesson course and, later, to use with my upcoming method books. I am a concert pianist and a university piano professor, and these are the kinds of tools I look at first when a student asks what to buy.

What makes a good piano bench?

  • Adjustable height, so your forearms can sit level with the key tops or slightly above.
  • A stable stand that does not flex when you play with weight.
  • Seat width. A duet bench seats a teacher beside a student.
  • Padding you can sit on for a full practice session.
  • Storage under the seat, if you keep books close at hand.

How does the right bench help you play better?

How this helps you learn: correct sitting height. Get the bench right and the forearm, wrist and sound improve at once, and every lesson of the free course becomes easier on the body.

What do adults ask about piano benches?

What height should a piano bench be?

Your forearms should be level with the key tops or slightly above when your arms hang loose and you bring them up to the keys. An adjustable bench lets you set that once and forget it. My article on bench height and posture shows the three-minute setup.

Should I buy a fixed or an adjustable bench?

An adjustable bench is the safer choice if more than one person plays, or if you are unsure of your height. A fixed bench works if it already puts your forearms at the right level.

Do I need a stand for a digital piano?

Check how your instrument is built. A keyboard without legs needs a solid stand, and a wobbling X-style stand can teach your hand to avoid using weight.

Can I use a dining chair at a keyboard?

It is nearly always too low, and a table is lower than a keyboard. A proper bench or an adjustable stand-and-bench set solves it permanently.
