A home digital piano is the one I picture when a student tells me they want a piano in the living room and not a keyboard on a stand. The cabinet carries its own pedals and keeps the instrument steady, the keys are weighted, and the whole thing looks like part of the house. It costs more than a portable, and it is the instrument I would buy once and keep. Each note below is based on what the listing states.

Why trust a concert pianist's picks?

I recommend this gear because it is what my adult students need to work through my free 41-lesson course and, later, to use with my upcoming method books. I am a concert pianist and a university piano professor, and these are the kinds of tools I look at first when a student asks what to buy.

What should I look for in a home digital piano?

  • Weighted, graded hammer keys. The touch is what you practice, so this comes first.
  • Three pedals in the cabinet, because the pedal is part of a piano and a cabinet should include it.
  • The room. Measure the wall. Compact upright-style cabinets exist for small apartments.
  • A real piano sound with a headphone jack, so you can practice late.
  • A bench in the price or in the budget. Check whether the listing includes one.

How does a home digital piano help you learn?

How this helps you learn: an instrument that stands ready in the room is the one you will sit down at every day, and daily practice is what moves an adult forward fastest.

How do I choose between a console and an upright-style digital piano?

A console digital piano is a flat-topped cabinet, a little like a desk with keys. An upright-style cabinet looks like a small acoustic upright. Both carry their own pedals and keep the keys at the right height. The difference is mostly taste and space: a compact upright-style model takes less floor and suits a corner of an apartment, and a slim console fits under a window. In both, check three things on the listing: the key action, the pedals and whether a bench comes with it.

What I would try first

If you can, press the keys in a shop. Play one note softly and one note loudly, and listen for a clear difference. Then sit at the cabinet and see where your knees fall under the keyboard: you should not have to push them against the sides.

What do adults ask before buying a home digital piano?

Is a console digital piano better than a portable one?

Not better in sound, but better in furniture. A console has its own stand and pedals and stays put, which suits a home. A portable moves easily, which suits a small flat or a player who travels.

Do home digital pianos include a bench?

Some listings include one and some do not. Read the listing, and plan for an adjustable bench if it is not included.

How wide is a digital piano cabinet?

Most are about the width of an 88-key keyboard plus the cabinet sides. Check the dimensions on the listing against your wall before you order.

Can I use headphones with a home digital piano?

Yes. Digital pianos have a headphone jack, which is the main reason many adults choose them over an acoustic piano in an apartment.
