A home studio kit is a way to start recording without choosing each piece alone. It usually contains an audio interface, a microphone and a way to monitor, and often some recording software. I include bundles and a few simpler alternatives, such as a handheld recorder or a USB microphone, because the best setup is the one an adult will really use every week. I describe each by its listing, and I ask you to read what is in the box before you buy.

Why trust a concert pianist's picks?

I recommend this gear because it is what my adult students need to work through my free 41-lesson course and, later, to use with my upcoming method books. I am a concert pianist and a university piano professor, and these are the kinds of tools I look at first when a student asks what to buy.

What should I look for in a home studio kit?

  • What is in the box. Read the listing for the interface, microphone, headphones, cables and software.
  • One microphone or two. A single microphone is fine for a voice or a mono clip; a stereo pair suits a piano.
  • Software. Check which recording software comes with it and whether it runs on your computer.
  • Room to grow. An interface with two inputs lets you add a second microphone later.
  • Simplicity. A handheld recorder or a USB microphone is a good first step if you do not want a computer in the room.

How does a recording kit help you learn?

How this helps you learn: a simple kit that is always ready makes recording a habit, and a recording is the most honest teacher you have between lessons.

What do students ask about home studio kits?

What do I need to start a home recording studio?

A computer or a recorder, a way to capture sound (an interface and microphone, or a USB microphone), headphones, and some recording software. A bundle packs most of these into one purchase.

Is a bundle better than buying pieces?

A bundle is simpler and often cheaper than buying each piece. Buying pieces gives you more choice of microphone and headphones.

Can I record a digital piano without a microphone?

Yes. A digital piano can connect straight to an interface or a computer by cable or USB, with no microphone.

Do I need a studio to send you a recording?

No. A phone is enough for my free feedback. A kit is for when you want to hear your playing as a listener does.

Shopping outside the US? Do these links work in the UK, Canada, Australia or Europe?

Yes. The links go to Amazon.com and open your local Amazon store when the model is sold there: the UK, Canada, Australia, Germany, France, Italy, Spain, the Netherlands, Poland, Sweden and Singapore.
