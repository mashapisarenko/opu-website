Questions about learning piano as an adult

Is there a best age to start learning piano?

The best age is the one you are in. Children and adults learn differently, and adults bring focus, patience and a clear idea of the music they love.

Can adults really learn piano from free online lessons?

Yes, if the lessons are in order and teach the sound and the hand, not only which key to press. The free course here has 41 lessons, and you can send a recording for feedback.

Will the website tell me which songs I can learn?

Each age page lists music from that decade. Tell me the one you want and I will point you to the lessons that lead to it.
