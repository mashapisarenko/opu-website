Questions about gift certificates

How does a piano lesson gift certificate work?

You fill in the order form and choose a package and a design. You receive a secure PayPal payment link or invoice by email. After payment, you receive the printable certificate by email within 24 hours. The recipient then books online lessons with Dr. Maria Pisarenko in their own time zone.

Can I still give a certificate at the last minute?

Yes. The certificate is delivered by email within 24 hours, so you can print it, or forward the email, for a last-minute gift.

Do I need a PayPal account to pay?

No. You receive a secure PayPal payment link or invoice by email, and any credit or debit card works, worldwide. No PayPal account is needed.

How long is a gift certificate valid?

There is no expiration date for starting: the recipient books the first lesson whenever they are ready. Once lessons have begun, a ten-lesson package is used within six months of the first lesson, and a four-lesson package within two months. A single lesson does not expire.
