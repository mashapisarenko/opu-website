I am Dr. Maria Pisarenko, a concert pianist with a Doctor of Musical Arts degree, trained in the Russian conservatory school at the Central Music School of the Moscow Tchaikovsky Conservatory and the Gnessin Russian Academy of Music. I teach piano at the college and university level, and this page is the whole of my free course on Alfred’s Adult All-in-One Course, Book 1: every lesson on video, in order, with the page numbers, so you can open the book and follow along.

How to use this page

  1. Open the book to the first page of the lesson. The page numbers are in the table. We follow the book page by page.
  2. Watch in order, Lesson 1 to 41. Each lesson builds on the one before, and nothing is skipped.
  3. Jump to any topic. Each lesson has chapters, so you can return to the exact moment you need.
  4. Practice with me. The Extra Practice playlist has eight close-up play-along sessions that accompany the course, so you can watch my hands from above and practice together at your pace.
  5. Send me your playing. You can send a recording and get written feedback while the pilot runs.

Prefer to see the lessons as one playlist? The free course page has the whole playlist, with a Start button.

Questions adults ask

Is Alfred's Adult All-in-One good for beginners?

It is the book I teach from, and it is the only book you need to start. If you want to judge any method book for yourself, use my ten-point checklist for choosing one: it covers print size, whether every symbol is explained before it appears, and whether the left hand is treated as an equal.

Can I learn from Alfred's book without a teacher?

Yes, but if you are learning on your own, your method book is doing a teacher's work. It decides what you learn first, what you learn next and what you never learn at all. A book with recordings and video helps, and the free course on this site shows each step at the piano. You can also send me a recording of your own playing and get written feedback while the pilot runs.

Do I need the book to follow the videos?

The lessons follow the book page by page, and the page numbers in the table above refer to it, so keep the book open on the music stand as you watch. It is the course book, and the only book you need to start. The first lesson covers the warm-up, posture and finding the keys.
