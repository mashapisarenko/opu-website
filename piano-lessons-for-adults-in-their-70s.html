Be honest about the obstacles

What is actually in your way past seventy

Hands that tire or stiffen

Short, gentle sessions work best, with rests built in. Warm hands first. If you have arthritis or any pain, ask your doctor what is right for you; I am a pianist, not a physician.

Eyesight and the size of the music

Large-print editions, good light and a music stand at the right height make a real difference. We start with very little notation and add only what you need.

Memory and repetition

Play a little, then rest, then play again tomorrow. A short notebook entry after each session keeps your place.

Feeling out of place in a world of apps

You do not need an app. A keyboard, headphones and a browser are enough. The lessons are videos that you can pause as often as you like.

Being told it is too late

It is not. You can learn to play a piece you love, and play it for your family. That is a real musical achievement.

Questions adults ask in their 70s

Can you learn piano at 75?

Yes. Adults of every age learn to play, and the course is designed to be paused, repeated and returned to. Your goal can be as simple as one favorite song.

Is playing piano good for older adults?

Many people find it enjoyable and calming. I am a pianist and a teacher, not a physician, so for any health question please ask your doctor.

What keyboard should an older beginner choose?

One with 88 weighted keys, a stable stand or bench and headphones if you want quiet. The gear page has my recommendations.

Are there online piano lessons for seniors over 70?

Yes. The free course here is unhurried, uses large clear music on screen, and you can pause and repeat any lesson. You can also send a recording and get a personal written answer from me.
