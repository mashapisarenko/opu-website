What should a UK buyer check?

Check the plug and the power supply. A digital piano bought for the UK should come with a UK plug or a suitable adapter, and the listing will say. Check delivery times for a large box, and decide where it will stand before it arrives. For furniture, note that a bench is called a piano stool in the UK, and a keyboard stand is sold separately. A sustain pedal is the same everywhere.

Questions adults ask

Do these links work in the UK?

Yes. They go to Amazon.com and open Amazon UK when the model is sold there. Check the page that opens is the model you expect.

What is a piano stool in the UK?

The same thing as a piano bench in the US. Choose an adjustable one so your forearms sit level with the keys.

Do I need a UK-plug digital piano?

You need the right power supply for UK sockets. The listing for your country will state what comes in the box; if it does not, buy a suitable adapter.
