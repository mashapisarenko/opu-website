Yamaha HS5 vs PreSonus Eris E5: A Pianist's Comparison (2026)

Two popular five-inch monitors at different prices. Here is what the listings say and how I would choose.
By Dr. Maria Pisarenko, DMA  ·  November 30, 2026

By Dr. Maria Pisarenko, DMA · Last updated: November 14, 2026 · Product details checked against the Amazon listing and the maker's page on that date.

Shopping for this? My picks, chosen by a concert pianist: Studio Monitors and Speakers.

The PreSonus Eris E5 is the better value for most pianists: Amazon lists a pair of 5.25-inch two-way near-field monitors at a lower cost than a pair of Yamaha HS5. The Yamaha HS5 is the one to choose if you want its plain, flat reputation and are willing to pay for it. Neither verdict is a measurement; the specifications in the table come from the makers' own pages, opened on November 16, 2026.

Quick verdict

  • Better value: PreSonus Eris E5 (pair) — a pair of 5.25-inch two-way near-field monitors
  • Plainer reputation: Yamaha HS5 (pair) — a matched pair of 5-inch monitors

Watts are not loudness and the two makers do not give the same figures, so I would not choose on this table alone; it tells you what each can connect to and how it is built.

How I would choose

  • If budget matters, the Eris E5 pair.
  • If you want the flattest sound by reputation, the HS5.
  • If you can, listen to both with a recording you know well.

PreSonus Eris E5 (pair)

Who it is for: A pianist who wants honest two-way monitors for a desk or a small room, bought as a pair.

What the listing says: The listing says a pair of 5.25-inch two-way near-field monitors. PreSonus lists a 5.25-inch woven-composite woofer, a 1-inch silk-dome tweeter, 80 W total Class AB, XLR, TRS and RCA inputs and a 102 dB peak SPL.

What I look for: A monitor should show you what your playing really sounds like, including the unflattering parts. I listen for a clear middle range, because that is where a piano's melody lives.

The one I would buy first for a pianist on a budget.

Pros
  • Sold as a pair
  • 5.25-inch two-way design
Cons
  • Read the full specifications on the maker's page, not just the listing
  • Needs a place on a desk or stands

Buy it if you want a pair that is simple to set up. Skip it if you will only listen on headphones.

Yamaha HS5 (pair)

Who it is for: A recordist who wants Yamaha's plain, honest monitors as a matched pair.

What the listing says: The listing says a matched pair of 5-inch monitors. Yamaha lists a 5-inch cone woofer, a 1-inch dome tweeter, 70 W bi-amplified (45 W low, 25 W high), a 54 Hz to 30 kHz response (-10 dB) and XLR and TRS inputs.

What I look for: The HS series has a reputation for a flat, unflattering response, which is what a teacher wants to hear.

The one I would choose for accuracy.

Pros
  • Matched pair
  • Plain, flat reputation
Cons
  • Read the full specifications on the maker's page, not just the listing
  • Higher cost than the Eris pair

Buy it if you want accuracy over flattery. Skip it if you want the lowest cost.

Questions adults ask

Which is better, the HS5 or the Eris E5?

For most pianists, the Eris E5 for value. The HS5 for a plainer reputation.

Do I need a pair?

Yes, for stereo. Both listings here are pairs.

Do I need monitors if I use headphones?

No. Monitors are for listening without headphones.
