Best Music Gifts Under $100 for Pianists (Teacher's Picks)

Six gifts under $100 that a pianist will use every week, with what to check on each before you buy.

Shopping for this? My picks, chosen by a concert pianist: Gifts for Pianists and Gifts for Music Lovers.

The best gifts under $100 for a pianist are the ones they will use every week: a light for the music stand, a metronome, a pair of closed-back headphones, a clip that holds a music book open, and a good book about music. When I checked on November 14, 2026, each item below was listed under $100 on Amazon; prices move, so check today's price before you order.

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. That never changes what I recommend: every item here is something I would tell my own adult students to look at. Check today's price on Amazon.

My top 3 picks

  • A light for the music desk: Mighty Bright Orchestra Light — an orchestra-style stand light
  • A dependable metronome: Korg KDM-3 Digital Metronome — a digital metronome with memory backup
  • A book to read: Musicophilia: Tales of Music and the Brain — a book on music and the brain

How I choose

I choose a gift for how often it will be used and how little it asks of the person receiving it. A gift that needs setting up, or that duplicates what they already own, becomes clutter. I have not played every instrument or used every device in this guide, and I do not say that I have. What I give you is what I look for as a concert pianist who teaches adults, checked against what each Amazon listing and manufacturer page actually states.

How to choose

  • If the pianist plays at night, the light or the headphones.
  • If they practice with a book, the clip.
  • If you do not know what they have, the book.
