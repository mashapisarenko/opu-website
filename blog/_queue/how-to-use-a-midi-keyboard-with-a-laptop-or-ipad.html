How to Use a MIDI Keyboard with a Laptop or iPad

A MIDI keyboard makes no sound on its own. Here is how to connect one to a laptop or an iPad, and which keyboard I would choose.
By Dr. Maria Pisarenko, DMA  ·  February 2, 2027

To use a MIDI keyboard with a laptop you usually need only the USB cable that comes with it. With an iPad you need the right adapter for the iPad's port, or a keyboard that connects by Bluetooth. Either way the keyboard sends notes to an app or program, and the app makes the sound. A MIDI keyboard on its own is silent.

What MIDI is, in one paragraph

MIDI is not sound. It is a message that says which key you pressed, how hard and for how long. The computer or tablet reads those messages and plays a piano sound it already holds. That is why a MIDI keyboard is called a controller, and why it needs software or an app. My guide weighted vs semi-weighted keys explains why the keys matter for a pianist.

With a laptop

  1. Plug the keyboard's USB cable into the laptop. Most laptops recognize it with no driver.
  2. Open the app or program you want to play, and check its settings for the MIDI input. Choose your keyboard.
  3. Play a note. If you hear nothing, check that the program has a piano sound loaded and that the laptop's volume is up.

With an iPad

  1. Check the iPad's port. Some have USB-C and need only a USB-C cable; older ones use a Lightning port and need an adapter.
  2. If the keyboard needs more power than the iPad can give, use a powered adapter or a powered USB hub.
  3. Open your app and choose the keyboard as the MIDI input.

I have not listed the exact adapter for each iPad here, because the right one depends on your iPad and your keyboard; check Apple's page for your model before you buy.

Which software should I use?

Start with a free program. Many piano-learning apps, notation programs and recording programs accept a MIDI keyboard. If you are learning from my free 41-lesson course, you do not need software at all; a MIDI keyboard becomes useful when you want to record yourself or use an app to practice reading notes.

Which keyboard should I buy?

If you want to learn piano repertoire on it, choose 88 keys with a weighted action. If you want a small desk keyboard for software only, 49 keys will do. A 25-key keyboard is a toy for a pianist; it is for sound design, not for learning to play. If a keyboard that makes its own sound is better for you, read the best 88-key weighted MIDI controller and my digital piano guide for adult beginners.

Questions adults ask

Do I need a driver for a MIDI keyboard?

Most laptops and tablets recognize a MIDI keyboard without one. Check the maker's page if yours does not appear.

Can I connect a MIDI keyboard to an iPad?

Yes, with the right cable or adapter for your iPad's port, or by Bluetooth if the keyboard supports it.

Why is my MIDI keyboard silent?

A MIDI keyboard makes no sound of its own. Open an app with a piano sound loaded and turn up the volume.
