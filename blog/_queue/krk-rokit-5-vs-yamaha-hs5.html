KRK Rokit 5 vs Yamaha HS5: A Pianist's Comparison (2026)

Shopping for this? My picks, chosen by a concert pianist: Studio Monitors and Speakers.

For monitoring piano I would choose the Yamaha HS5 if you want the plainest, least flattering sound, and the KRK Rokit 5 G5 if you want the more familiar, bass-forward character it is known for. Both are 5-inch powered monitors. Amazon lists the KRK as a pair and the HS5 as a single speaker or as a matched pair, so check which you are buying.

What I listen for in a monitor for piano

  • The middle. A piano's melody lives between roughly the middle of the keyboard and the treble; a monitor must keep it clear.
  • The bass. A monitor that flatters the bass makes you play the left hand too softly in your own recordings.
  • The treble. Harshness here tires your ears.
