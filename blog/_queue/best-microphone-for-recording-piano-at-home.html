Best Microphone for Recording Piano at Home

Hear what a listener hears, and send it to a teacher: simple, affordable ways to record yourself at the piano.
By Dr. Maria Pisarenko, DMA  ·  November 21, 2026

Forty-one lessons in order, free, at your own pace: Start the free course →

For recording piano at home, the simplest good choice is a handheld stereo recorder such as the Zoom H1n, or a cardioid USB condenser microphone such as the Audio-Technica AT2020USB-X. Place it a little above and in front of the instrument, record one short passage, and listen back.

Why should I record myself playing piano?

Recording yourself is the quickest way to hear what a listener hears. You notice uneven rhythm, rushed endings and notes you did not mean to play. It is also how you can send your playing to me for feedback: I read every message and listen to every recording myself, and you can send yours on the feedback page. A clear clip is all I need to hear your touch and rhythm; it does not have to be perfect.

Where should I put the microphone?

Start with the microphone a little above and in front of the instrument, record one short passage, and listen back. If the notes are clear and not boomy, your setup is good. If it sounds thin, move the microphone a little closer to the sound; if it sounds boomy or muddy, move it back. For an upright or a digital piano with speakers, aim at the speakers or the sound source, not at the wall.

Can I record a digital piano without a microphone?

Yes, and it is often the cleanest option. Yamaha lists a USB audio interface on the P-145BT (44.1 kHz, 16-bit, stereo) and a 24-bit one on the P-225, and Roland lists USB MIDI/audio on the FP-30X, so the piano can send its sound straight to a computer. The headphone jack can also feed a recorder. This avoids room noise entirely. I describe the pianos in the best digital piano for adult beginners.

How do I film my hands on the keys?

If you want to show your hands, a phone on an overhead mount looks down at the keys. The heavy-duty aluminum overhead phone mount and the SmallRig magic arm clamp are two ways; the UBeesize ring light with overhead phone mount adds light. Even light matters: a small LED panel such as the NEEWER 9 inch LED video light helps. A webcam such as the Logitech C920x works for live lessons over video. The sound still comes best from a separate recorder or microphone, not the phone.

What about a wireless clip-on microphone?

It is useful if you also speak to the camera, as a teacher does, but it is not the right tool for recording piano. For talking, the DJI Mic Mini is a compact wireless system in my shop list.

What mistakes spoil a piano recording?

Three, mostly. Placing the microphone too far away, so the room is louder than the piano. Recording next to a noisy appliance or a window. And pressing record only when you are ready to play perfectly, so that you never record the honest, ordinary version. Record the ordinary version. The point is information, not performance, and a teacher can use a rough take far better than a polished one that hides the problem. Keep recordings short, name each file with the date and the piece, and you will have a diary of progress in a few months.

How do I send a recording to be heard?

Record one short passage, ideally the piece you are working on, with a few seconds of silence before and after. Save it as a common audio or video file and send it on the feedback page. If you are not ready to send, record for yourself. Listening back is part of practice, and your ear is the best teacher you have between lessons.

Questions adults ask

What is the best microphone for recording piano?

For beginners, a handheld stereo recorder like the Zoom H1n or a cardioid USB condenser microphone like the Audio-Technica AT2020USB-X.

Can I record piano with my phone?

Yes, for a feedback clip. A separate recorder or microphone gives clearer sound than a phone placed far from the instrument.

How do I record a digital piano?

Use its USB audio connection to a computer, or record with a microphone or recorder placed in front of the speakers.

Where should I put the microphone for piano?

A little above and in front of the instrument. Record a short passage, listen back, and move it until the sound is clear.

How can I get feedback on my piano playing?

Record a short clip and send it on the feedback page at Online Piano University.
