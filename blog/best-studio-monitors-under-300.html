Quick picks

  • Best overall: PreSonus Eris E5 (pair) — a pair of 5.25-inch two-way near-field monitors
  • Best for a small desk: M-Audio BX4 (pair) — a pair of 4.5-inch powered monitors
  • Best for streaming too: M-Audio BX5BT (pair) — 5-inch desktop monitors with Bluetooth

How I choose

A monitor should tell you the truth about your playing. I check size, whether the listing is for a pair, and what the maker says about its use. I have not played every instrument or used every device in this guide, and I do not say that I have. What I give you is what I look for as a concert pianist who teaches adults, checked against what each Amazon listing and manufacturer page actually states.

The picks

PreSonus Eris E5 (pair)

Who it is for: A pianist who wants honest two-way monitors for a desk or a small room, bought as a pair.

What the listing says: The listing says a pair of 5.25-inch two-way near-field monitors. PreSonus lists a 5.25-inch woven-composite woofer, a 1-inch silk-dome tweeter, 80 W total Class AB, XLR, TRS and RCA inputs and a 102 dB peak SPL.

What I look for: A monitor should show you what your playing really sounds like, including the unflattering parts. I listen for a clear middle range, because that is where a piano's melody lives.

The one I would pick for a pianist who wants to hear the middle of the piano clearly.

M-Audio BX4 (pair)

Who it is for: A small desk where a keyboard line out goes to two compact monitors.

What the listing says: The listing says a pair of 4.5-inch powered monitors for a desk.

What I look for: Small monitors give less bass; I would pair them with headphones for the lowest octaves.

For a very small desk.

How to choose

  • Make sure the listing is for a pair, as these are. Many monitors are sold singly.
  • Place them at ear height, forming a triangle with your head.
  • If you only use headphones, you do not need monitors: see headphones for digital piano.

Questions adults ask

Do I need studio monitors for a digital piano?

Not if you play on headphones. They help when you want to hear recordings or share the sound.

Are these sold as pairs?

Yes, the three here are listed as pairs. Many monitors on Amazon are sold singly.

What cables do I need?

Check the monitor inputs and your piano's outputs; see my shop page on music cables.
