Christmas Gifts for Musicians: A Pianist's List

Gifts that serve the music, from a metronome to a recording to a lesson with a teacher.
By Dr. Maria Pisarenko, DMA  ·  November 10, 2026

Forty-one lessons in order, free, at your own pace: Start the free course →

The best Christmas gifts for musicians are useful at the instrument: headphones, a metronome or tuner, a music stand and light, quality books and scores, and a recorder. For something special, give a great recording, a fine pen or a gift certificate for lessons. Christmas falls on Friday, December 25, 2026.

What are the best Christmas gifts for a musician?

Things that make practice better. Headphones, a metronome, a stand, a light, a recorder. These are the gifts that a musician rarely buys for themselves and is glad to receive. After that come books and scores, then listening gifts, then personal accessories.

What books and scores make a good Christmas gift for a musician?

Match the repertoire to the player. For a pianist: Chopin Nocturnes, Bach's Well-Tempered Clavier, Part I, Beethoven Piano Sonatas, Volume I, or the Burgmuller 25 Progressive Pieces for an earlier stage. For someone starting, Alfred's Basic Adult All-in-One Course, Book 1. More are on the books and sheet music page.

What are good listening and gifts for a music lover?

The Bose QuietComfort headphones (2nd Gen) and the Bowers and Wilkins Px8 are listening headphones. For a larger tier, the Audeze LCD-X and the beyerdynamic DT 1990 Pro MKII are the luxury headphones in my shop list; check the makers' pages for their details. For recordings, the Glenn Gould Bach box and the Michelangeli set. For a fountain pen on a composer's desk, the Montblanc Meisterstuck Classique is a luxury choice.

What are personal Christmas gifts for a musician?

A pair of treble clef cufflinks, a sterling silver musical note necklace, a Heroic Polonaise sheet-music print, a piano music-note blanket or a wooden piano 3D puzzle for a quiet evening. Browse the gifts for pianists page.

How should I wrap a musician's gift?

Use sheet music as wrapping paper if you can find a spare page, and tuck a handwritten card inside naming a favorite piece. For a gift certificate, print the note and slip it into a book of pieces. A musician notices the thought as much as the item, and a card that names a piece you remember them playing is the best gift tag there is. If you are posting a gift, check delivery times, and have a backup, such as a lesson certificate, in case a package is late.

What is a good big gift for a pianist?

A weighted digital piano if they do not own one, such as the Yamaha P-145BT, or a bench and pedal if they do. See the best digital piano for adult beginners and the digital pianos page.

How do I choose a gift if I do not play myself?

Listen for what the musician complains about. If it is the neighbors, headphones. If it is a dim room, a light. If it is losing the beat, a metronome. If it is a page that slides off the music rest, a book clip. Gifts that solve a complaint are never forgotten. When you cannot tell, a book of repertoire for their instrument or a gift certificate lets them choose, and it saves you from guessing the edition or the level.

What is the best gift of all?

Questions adults ask

What do you buy a musician for Christmas?

Something that helps them practice: headphones, a metronome, a stand and light, a recorder, or music books. For something special, a recording or lessons.

What is a good gift for a pianist at Christmas?

A piano-style pedal, an adjustable bench, headphones, or a weighted digital piano. A gift certificate for lessons is the most valuable.

What is a good Christmas gift for a music teacher?

A music stand light, a metronome, a quality score, or a personal accessory with a handwritten note.

What if I do not know what instrument they play?

Choose headphones, a recorder, a book about music or a great recording. These suit most musicians.

When is Christmas 2026?

Friday, December 25, 2026.
