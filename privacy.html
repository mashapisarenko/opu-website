Privacy Policy

What I collect, why, and what happens to it — in plain language.

Last updated: 6 September 2026

This policy explains, in plain language, what Online Piano University collects, why, and what happens to it. Online Piano University is operated by Dr. Maria Pisarenko in Las Vegas, Nevada, United States. If anything here is unclear, write to maria@onlinepianouniversity.com and I will answer personally.

The short version. I collect your email address if you ask for the Starter Guide, and your recording if you send your playing for feedback. Recordings are used only to prepare your feedback — they are never published, never shared, and never sold. You can ask me to delete anything at any time.

1. What I collect

Email address

Collected only when you enter it yourself — to receive the free Adult Beginner’s Piano Starter Guide, or to receive feedback on a recording you sent. I do not buy lists and I do not add anyone who did not ask.

Recordings of your playing

If you use the Get Feedback form, you send an audio or video file of yourself playing, the lesson number you played, and optionally a note about what you found difficult. Video is normally hands-and-keyboard only; audio alone is always acceptable.

Messages you send

Anything you write to me by email, including your name and whatever you choose to tell me about your playing.

Basic technical data

Like any website, this one is served by a host that records standard request information such as IP address, browser type and the page requested. If website analytics are enabled, aggregate statistics about visits are collected — how many people read a page, which pages they came from — not who you are.

I do not collect payment card details on this website. If you buy lessons, payment is handled entirely by the payment provider on their own secure pages; I never see your card number.

2. Why I use it

  • To send you the Starter Guide you requested, and occasional lessons and practice tips.
  • To listen to or watch your recording and write your feedback.
  • To answer your questions and arrange lessons.
  • To understand which lessons and articles are useful, in aggregate.

That is the complete list. I do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose you did not ask for.

3. How feedback on your playing is prepared

Your recording is analyzed with the help of an AI assistant built on my own teaching method, and during this pilot I personally read and approve every piece of feedback before it reaches you. Your recording is processed only for that purpose. It is not used to train any public AI model, it is not published, and it is not shared with anyone else.

4. Who else touches your data

Only the services needed to run the site and deliver what you asked for:

  • GitHub Pages — hosts this website.
  • Render — runs the feedback engine that receives your recording.
  • Anthropic — the AI assistant used to help draft your feedback letter.
  • Brevo and Kit — deliver email, including your feedback letter and the Starter Guide.
  • Google Fonts — serves the typefaces used on these pages.
  • Google Analytics — aggregate visit statistics, if enabled.
  • A payment provider — if you purchase lessons, they handle the transaction and hold the payment details, not me.

I do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to anyone, for any reason.

5. Amazon and other affiliate links

Some pages link to instruments and books on Amazon. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases, at no extra cost to you. Amazon sets its own cookies when you follow such a link; what Amazon does then is governed by Amazon’s privacy policy, not this one. I only recommend things I actually use and teach with.

6. Cookies and storage

This site uses cookies and browser storage sparingly: to keep the site working offline where your browser supports it, and — where analytics are enabled — to count visits. There is no advertising tracking on this site. You can block or delete cookies in your browser at any time; the course and the pages will still work.

7. How long things are kept

  • Recordings: kept only as long as needed to prepare and send your feedback, and deleted afterwards on request — always within twelve months.
  • Email address: kept until you unsubscribe. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • Correspondence: kept as long as it is useful for teaching you, then deleted on request.

8. Your rights

Wherever you live, you may ask me to show you what I hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop emailing you. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you also have the rights granted by the GDPR, including the right to object and the right to data portability, and you may complain to your national supervisory authority. If you are a California resident, the CCPA gives you the right to know, delete, and opt out of any sale of personal information — and I want to be plain about this: I do not sell personal information at all.

To exercise any of this, write to maria@onlinepianouniversity.com. I will respond within thirty days, usually far sooner.

9. Children

This website and its free course are intended for adults. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through this site. If a parent or guardian believes a child has sent me something, write to me and I will delete it.

10. International transfers

I work from the United States, and the services listed above are largely based there. If you write to me from outside the US, your information will be processed in the US and other countries where those providers operate.

11. Security

The site is served over HTTPS and access to recordings and email addresses is limited to me. No system is perfectly secure, so please do not send me anything you would not want stored on an ordinary computer.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes will be announced by email to subscribers.
