Questions adults ask before they start

Is this piano course really free?

Yes. All forty-one lessons are on YouTube, in order, with no sign-up and no payment. You can also send a recording of your own playing and get written feedback while the pilot runs.

Is it too late to learn piano as an adult?

No. Some things are harder for an adult and some are easier, and neither decides the outcome. What decides it is whether you are taught the sound and the hand from the first lesson, instead of only which key to press.

Do I need a real piano, or is a keyboard enough?

A keyboard is enough to start, and two things matter more than the brand: eighty-eight keys if you can, and weighted keys, so your hand learns what a real instrument feels like.

How long before I can play a real piece?

You play a real piece on the first day of this method - the opening of Ode to Joy, by ear, with five fingers that never move. Reading music comes after that, not before it.

Do I need to read music to start?

No. The course starts with sound and with the hand, and reading is introduced step by step once you already know what you are listening for.

Who teaches the course?
